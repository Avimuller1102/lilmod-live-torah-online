# LILMOD.fr

**LILMOD.fr** is a non-profit platform that has been broadcasting live Torah classes since 2015. Its goal is to deliver Jewish teachings relevant to today's world, in the context of the return of the Jewish people to the Land of Israel.

## Features

- 🎥 **Live Torah Classes** — Over 10 live sessions per week with interactive Q&A.
- 📺 **Video Archive** — All classes are recorded and available on YouTube.
- 🎧 **Audio Podcast** — Listen on Spotify anytime, anywhere.
- 📅 **Weekly Program** — Join our WhatsApp group or subscribe to our newsletter to receive weekly schedules.
- 🧑‍🏫 **Diverse Teachers** — A range of Rabbanim and scholars across different fields of Torah study.

## Weekly Live Schedule

- **Sunday** — Jewish Philosophy with Rav Botshko
- **Monday** — Kol HaTor with Rav Akerman & Parasha with Rav Elie Kling
- **Tuesday** — Ethics of War with Rav Akerman
- **Wednesday** — Rav Kook's Writings with Rav Ben-Shoushan
- **Thursday** — Diaspora & Israel Relations with Rav Morali
- **Friday** — Weekly Parasha with Rav Ben Ichay
